Common questions

How do I remove a photo?
Tap it once — a small toolbar appears above it with a remove button.
How do I swap two photos?
Touch and hold one to pick it up, then drop it on the other. Crops travel with the photo.
How do I resize photos against each other?
Drag any line between them.
How do I reframe a photo inside its cell?
Drag inside it, or pinch to zoom. Automatic framing turns itself off for that photo and respects your choice.
Where does my export go?
Straight into your Photos library at 2160 px — and you can share it anywhere from the confirmation screen.
Are my original photos changed?
Never. Mosaico only ever writes the new image you export.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Mosaico collects no data. None.

  • The app never reads your photo library. The system photo picker hands it only the photos you explicitly select.
  • Face and subject detection run entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded — the app makes no network connections at all.
  • The only thing Mosaico ever writes outside itself is the finished image you export, saved to your Photos library with your permission.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party services.

If this policy ever changes, the change will be listed here before it takes effect.
